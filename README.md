# Advanced Industrial Organisation — UCPH

Projects from the MSc course *Advanced Industrial Organisation* (2026) at the University of Copenhagen.

The projects explore empirical industrial organisation, including demand estimation, market competition, merger analysis, and consumer search.

## Projects

### Project 1 — Car Demand and Merger Simulation

Estimation of car demand using logit and random-coefficients models (BLP), followed by an analysis of a hypothetical VW–Fiat merger, including price effects, substitution patterns, and consumer welfare.

[Project 1 (PDF)](p1_merger/AIO_project1_merger.pdf)

### Project 2 — Consumer Search on Expedia

Analysis of how hotel rankings affect search and bookings on Expedia. The project uses booking and click data to separate hotel appeal from search costs, then estimates how clicks and bookings would change if hotels were ranked randomly or by their estimated appeal.

[Project 2 (PDF)](p2_search/project2.pdf)
