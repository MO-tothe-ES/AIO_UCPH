# Advanced Industrial Organisation — UCPH

Projects from the MSc course *Advanced Industrial Organisation* (2026) at the University of Copenhagen.

The projects explore empirical industrial organisation, including demand estimation, market competition, and merger analysis.

## Projects

### Project 1 — Car Demand and Merger Simulation

Estimation of car demand using logit and random-coefficients models (BLP), followed by an analysis of a hypothetical VW–Fiat merger, including price effects, substitution patterns, and consumer welfare.
